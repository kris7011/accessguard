The problem

One missing check is enough

A handler that loads a record by id without checking the caller's tenant exposes every customer's records. Broken object level authorization is listed first in the OWASP API Security Top 10 (2023).

Hard to test by hand

With 3 tenants, 2 roles and 20 endpoints there are already well over 100 combinations to try, again after every change, and the dangerous ones are the ones nobody thought of.

Fixes break other things

A quick fix often blocks too much and breaks a legitimate share. A retest has to check the denial and the access that should still work.

How it works

1 · DeclareTest users, the records they own and who may read what. Import endpoints from OpenAPI; forms, no JSON.
2 · Approve and scanYou review the exact scope. A runner in your environment sends only GET requests to the targets you allow, within a budget and deadline.
3 · EvidenceA violation counts only when the protected values of another tenant's record appear in the response. Expired sessions, errors and login pages are never counted as passed.
4 · Fix and retestFix guidance per finding, an optional AI explanation, and a one-click retest that also checks legitimate access. History and triage per finding.

Why teams can trust the results

Honest status

Green means every declared check passed, nothing more. Partial scans, invalid test sessions and inconclusive responses are shown as incomplete, never as secure.

Your credentials stay with you

The runner reads test credentials locally and uploads a report with status codes, field names and hashes, never response content or credentials.

Scope you control twice

You approve every scan in the app, and the runner refuses any target that is not on its own local allowlist. Cloud metadata and link-local addresses are always blocked.

AI where it saves you work

Drafts from your OpenAPI document and plain-language explanations of findings. Verdicts, scope and budgets are decided by the deterministic engine, never by the model.

What AccessGuard does not do

It tests read access for the users, records and endpoints you declare. It does not crawl, test write operations, find injection bugs or certify an application as secure. Full list of limitations.
