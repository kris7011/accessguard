Questions

What is a check? One test user (or an anonymous request) reading one record or list. Three tenants with two roles and ten records give about 70 checks.

What happens when I hit a limit? New scans are refused with a clear message; existing results stay available.

Is this a security certification? No. AccessGuard verifies the access rules you declare for the endpoints you list. See limitations.
